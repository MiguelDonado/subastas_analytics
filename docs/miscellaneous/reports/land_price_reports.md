# Land price reports

Public studies on the price of rustic land in Spain.

## Farmland and forest

### Xunta de Galicia, yearly report on rustic land prices
- **What it gives:** prices of rustic land in Galicia, 2024 report.

## Farmland only

### Ministry of Agriculture, yearly survey of farmland prices
- **URL:** https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/estadistica/temas/estadisticas-agrarias/economia/tierra-precios
- **What it gives:** average prices of farmland, 2025 results.
- **Problem:** nothing on forest land.

## Forest only

### Fourth National Forest Inventory
- **URL:** https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/biodiversidad/temas/inventarios-nacionales/inventario-forestal-nacional/cuarto_inventario.html
- **What it gives:** one publication per region. Only Galicia's is downloaded.
